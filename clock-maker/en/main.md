# Clock Maker

An analog clock is a circular timepiece consisting of an hour hand and a minute hand. The clock face features numbers from 1 to 12. As time passes, the minute hand moves continuously around the circle, completing a full rotation every 60 minutes. Meanwhile, the hour hand moves continuously, completing a full rotation every 12 hours. Write a function to calculate the angles of the hour hand and the minute hand at a given input time.

## Test Cases

```ruby
clock_arms_radius("09:00")
> [270, 0]

clock_arms_radius("09:30")
> [285, 180]

clock_arms_radius("12:10")
> [5, 60]

clock_arms_radius("20:41")
> [260.5, 246]
```
